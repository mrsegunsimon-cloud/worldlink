Delete your account

Last updated: 2 November 2026

You can delete your WorldLink account and the data linked to it at any time.

In the app or website

  1. Open WorldLink and log in.
  2. Go to Profile, tap ☰, then Delete my account.
  3. Type your password and tap Delete everything.

Open WorldLink

What is deleted

Your sign-in, profile, videos, posts, stories, series and episodes, music and listings you added, messages, notifications, followers and following, saved email address and phone-alert settings. Deletion starts immediately. Cached copies can take a short time to disappear.

What may be kept

Payment and gift records that we must keep by law, and reports about rule-breaking, for as long as required. Coins you have not used and gifts cannot be refunded after deletion.

Cannot log in?

Email hello@worldlink.app from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify and delete your account within 30 days.
