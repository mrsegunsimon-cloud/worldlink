Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

WorldLink ("we", "us") is a social video app and website at worldlink.app, run from Nigeria. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and the choices you have. Questions: hello@worldlink.app.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address (or phone number if you sign in by phone), your name and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.
  • Profile and content you post: profile picture, bio, videos, photos, posts, stories, series, music, listings, events, comments, likes, follows and gifts.
  • Messages: private chats and group chats, including voice messages, photos and videos you send.
  • Payments: when you buy coins or boost a video, payment is taken by Paystack. We receive a confirmation and the amount, not your card number. Gifts and coin balances are kept in your account.
  • Device permissions you allow: camera and microphone (to record videos, calls and voice messages), photos and files (to upload), location (only when you use "near me" features, and only at that moment), and notifications.
  • Technical data: basic usage and error information such as device type, app version and approximate region, used to keep the service working and fight abuse.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: show your content, deliver messages, calls, gifts and alerts.
  • To send you email about your account, and updates from WorldLink (you can unsubscribe in any update email).
  • To keep people safe: handle reports, block abuse and enforce our Terms.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not show third-party advertising profiles built from it.

3. Who can see what

Videos, posts, series, profile and comments you publish are visible to other WorldLink users and, for videos, may be visible to anyone with the link. Private chats are visible only to the people in them. You can use Privacy settings in the app to control who finds and contacts you, and you can block or report any account.

4. Service providers

We use trusted providers to run WorldLink: Google Firebase (sign-in and database), Supabase (file storage and alerts), Cloudflare (website delivery and security), Resend (email), Paystack (payments), and web push services of your browser or phone maker (alerts). Music search may query Audius and Apple iTunes, and some videos play from YouTube; those services have their own policies. Data may be processed in countries outside Nigeria.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account (see below), we remove your profile, content, messages, followers and saved email and alert settings. Payment records may be kept as long as the law requires. Backups and caches can take a short time to clear.

6. Your rights and choices

  • See and edit your profile in the app.
  • Delete your account and data: Profile → ☰ → Delete my account, or see worldlink.app/delete-account.html.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, correction or deletion at hello@worldlink.app. We reply within 30 days.
  • Turn off notifications in the app, your browser or your phone settings.
  • Withdraw permissions (camera, microphone, location) in your phone settings at any time.

We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. Children

WorldLink is for people aged 13 and over (or the minimum age in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect data from younger children. If you think a child has an account, email us and we will remove it.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is protected by access rules in our database. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will tell you in the app or by email. The date at the top shows the latest version.
